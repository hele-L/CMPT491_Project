INCOME3 and _HLTHPL2 additionally show a separate historical table for 2019-2020, since those two switched to a genuinely different coding scheme in 2021. _RACE's 2022 column is sourced from that year's comparable _RACE1 variable, not the separate, further-collapsed _RACEGR4 (see the note on that page). Percentages are omitted — frequency counts only. The dot next to each column in the sidebar shows its verification status (see legend below).
